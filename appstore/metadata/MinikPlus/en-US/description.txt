Little games. Big discoveries.

Minik Plus turns Hebrew and English practice into short, playful games. Meet Minik, the rainbow unicorn cat, hear every word spoken aloud and practice letters, first sounds, spelling and everyday vocabulary at your child's own pace.

TWO LANGUAGES IN ONE APP
On a device in English, or in any language other than Hebrew, Minik Plus starts with Hebrew practice. On a Hebrew device it starts with English. Switch the practiced language whenever you like in the Parents area.

Made for families abroad, new arrivals in Israel, Hebrew school and day school students, bilingual homes and anyone starting the Alef-Bet from scratch.

MANY WAYS TO PRACTICE
• Learn: meet a new word with a picture and its spoken sound.
• Picture to Word and Word to Picture: listen, read and choose.
• First Letter and Picture Starters: find the sound a word begins with.
• Build and Word Build: put the letters in order and spell the word.
• Pairs, Letter Pairs and Memory: match pictures, words and letters.
• Word Cards: review new words at a glance.
• Mixed practice: a little of everything in one round.

LEARNING THAT FEELS LIKE PLAY
• Soccer: kick the right letters into the goal, in order, to spell the word.
• Alphabet Blocks: stack letter blocks into a word tower.
• Tic-Tac-Toe against Minik for a quick break.

955 EVERYDAY WORDS
More than 30 topics: animals, colors, shapes, numbers, fruit and vegetables, food, the body, feelings, home, school, sports, transport, jobs, music, countries and more.

LEVELS THAT GROW WITH YOUR CHILD
Choose the word level yourself, or let Minik Plus adjust it automatically from recent answers.

A PROFILE FOR EVERY CHILD
Add up to 20 children, each with their own name, points, streaks and progress. The Progress screen shows what was practiced and for how long, and stays on this device.

POINTS, STREAKS AND THE TOP 20
Correct answers earn points and build streaks. Want a challenge? Join the Top 20 with a nickname.

MENUS IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Menus and instructions follow your device's language: English, Hebrew, Russian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Arabic or Amharic.
