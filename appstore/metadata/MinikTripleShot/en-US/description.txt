Think. Aim. Shoot.

Build your team and find your shot on colorful basketball courts. Play one-on-one, pass and screen in 2v2 or 3v3, or challenge a friend to match your shot in HORSE.

BASKETBALL YOU CAN READ
Swipe to move, tap a teammate to pass, and hold open court before releasing a shot. Team basketball has a 24-second shot clock, rebounds and out-of-bounds turnovers. Move close to the ball handler and tap the court to try a steal or block. Drive close for a layup or dunk. Distance, defense, timing and character abilities all affect your chance of scoring.

YOUR TEAM, YOUR LEAGUE
Choose a name, color and lineup. Play a local league against varied house teams. Choose first to 11 points, or a 2-, 3- or 5-minute match. A tied timed match continues until the next basket. Return to the league table to follow your saved season, compare player abilities and see your team in its chosen color.

12 CHARACTERS WITH PERSONALITY
Choose from Maya, Flare, Gaya, Kyra, Comet and more. Compare shooting, speed, handling and defense, then build a team that suits your style. Sonic joins the roster with his own abilities. Winged players fold their wings on the ground and spread them in the air.

LEARN ONE SKILL AT A TIME
The daily skill journey practices movement, passing, shooting, defense and teamwork on the real court. The 23-stage journey includes crossovers, behind-the-back dribbles and spins.

HORSE AND SHOOTING CHALLENGES
Set a shot for your opponent to match. Choose the visible aiming arc or a jump-shot style without the arc. Try the timed Shooting Challenge, answer questions and select a matching answer on court. Learning subjects bring another way to practice focus and decision-making. In team play, review the highlighted correct answer and continue when you are ready. HORSE also highlights answer feedback and keeps an Exit game action available during review.

SUNSET COURTS AND COURT SOUNDS
Play with a moving camera, basketball bounce and catch sounds, and net feedback. Swipe left-right-left to change hands, repeat for a behind-the-back dribble, or draw a circle to try a spin past your guard. Teammates can screen a close defender. Menu music, court ambience and crowd reactions bring the courts to life. Choose portrait or landscape where supported. Game sounds can be switched off in Settings.
