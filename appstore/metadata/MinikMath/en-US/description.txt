A little practice. A colorful companion. Another reason to try.

Minik Math turns everyday arithmetic into short, playful rounds. Join Minik for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, then take a paddle-game break with number questions along the way.

FOUR WAYS TO PRACTICE
Choose the operation to work on: add, subtract, multiply or divide. Practice the times tables from 1 to 9 and come back to the numbers that need a little more attention.

SHORT ROUNDS THAT FIT YOUR DAY
Every round has 10 questions. Fit in a quick round, focus on one skill and come back for more when you are ready.

ROOM TO TRY AGAIN
Clear feedback after every answer. A wrong answer is a chance to have another go, with encouraging Minik reactions along the way. At the end, see how you did and choose to practice again or try another number.

A COMFORTABLE LEVEL
Easy, Medium and Hard let you set the challenge. Minik Math remembers your level, so the next round starts the way you like it.

TAKE A BOUNCE & LEARN BREAK
The included retro Pong game mixes paddle action with arithmetic trains. Move with the on-screen arrows or swipe, keep the ball in play and tap a wagon to answer the train's question. Turn on Balloon Madness for extra colorful objects and lively collisions.

ENGLISH AND HEBREW
Switch between English and Hebrew, with a clear layout and cheerful character animations. A quick round at home, another go at a tricky times table or a playful moment of learning together.

Choose an operation, meet Minik and make time for a little math.
