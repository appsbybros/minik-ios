A gentle first rally. A daring diagonal shot. A rival who makes you try again.

Minik Ping Pong brings colorful table tennis to your phone. Learn with Minik, choose a house player with a style of their own, or share a private code and play a friend.

A LITTLE PRACTICE, A BETTER RALLY
Follow a daily skill journey with 30 stages: place your serves, return to moving targets, paint shapes, defend your half and learn to play with a teammate. Your first completed stage each day earns a bonus. Play more whenever you like; missing a day never erases your progress. Finish the journey and begin another cycle with challenges matched to the skills you are learning.

TWO TOGETHER, TWO WAYS TO PLAY
Choose a house teammate and face two house opponents. Take turns hitting, or let either teammate return. Each player covers their own side of the table. Learn positioning and timing together, with team exercises in the daily journey.

You can also create a private online team room: invite a friend to your side, play on opposite teams with a house partner each, or fill all four places with friends. Copy or share the room code. Friends bring their own nicknames and avatars. The room creator taps players to arrange the teams or choose house players. Start when every place is filled and your selected friends are connected.

FIND YOUR FEEL FOR THE BALL
Beginner helps you get started: move the paddle into position and the game times the hit. Standard controls let you tap for a natural return or swipe to guide the ball across the table. Pro controls ask for more precise timing, direction and power. Your control choice is remembered, and it is separate from the rival you choose.

MEET YOUR NEXT RIVAL
Minik offers a friendly introduction. Kyra, Flare, Gaya, Mia and the other house players bring different power, forehand, backhand and serving skills. Watch their placement and pace, protect your side of the table and find the opening for your next shot.

PLAY A FRIEND
Create a friendly match and share its room code. Invite a friend for a head-to-head match. Choose a playful nickname and avatar, then see your chosen character take part in the match. Online play needs an internet connection.

MAKE IT A PRIVATE TOURNAMENT
Bring friends and house players into the same competition. Follow the schedule, check the standings and return to an unfinished tournament when you are ready to continue.

LEARN BY DOING
The interactive guide pairs demonstrations with short practice exercises. Try a serve, place it to the left or right, return a simple ball and experiment with diagonal shots. Clear feedback helps you see what your gesture changed.

Bright courts and expressive characters bring every rally to life. Start with the basics, explore a different opponent and enjoy the moment when your next shot lands just where you wanted.
