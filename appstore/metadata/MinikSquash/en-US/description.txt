Solve it. Aim it. Keep the rally alive.

Minik Squash brings racket action and playful math together on a bright, spacious court. Questions appear on the front wall with four answer circles. Find the answer, choose your target and return the ball before its second bounce.

TWO WAYS TO SCORE
Win match points when your opponent faults: a missed return, two bounces, a low shot, an out ball or a shot that hits the floor before the front wall. Correct answers earn separate learning points, even when you lose the rally. Simply hitting the wall does not win a match point.

TIME TO THINK
Choose questions on your own serves, on every serve, or on every return with slow motion. Select 5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds to answer. Full-time serve questions give everyone their answer window; a quicker option closes when the serve begins. A grown-up can enable play without questions in the parent zone.

YOUR SHOT, YOUR WAY
Beginner moves your player toward the ball; tap your wall target as it arrives, with forgiving timing. Standard lets you move and aim with a timed tap. Pro puts direction and power in your swipe through the ball. Learn each action with Minik in an interactive, ad-free lesson.

FAMILIAR FACES, FRESH CHALLENGES
Pick your avatar and a house opponent, or play a saved three-match local cup. Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, order of operations and fractions. Questions adapt by skill, and clear feedback helps with the next attempt.

Good returns, answers and wins build your total points and your avatar's rank color, from the first steps toward silver and gold. Save an unfinished local match and return later.

Play offline in portrait or on the wide landscape court, on phones and tablets. Ads appear only between matches, never during a rally or lesson.

DAILY SKILLS, REAL RALLIES

Practice aiming, placement and returns in short daily challenges. Learn one skill, combine it with another, and keep progressing at your own pace.

PLAY TOGETHER

Try a match or a short competition, with house players filling places where the mode allows it.
