Follow Minik into 100 colorful 3D mazes with branching paths, water balloons, helpful surprises and a friendly rival. Read the question on the floor and walk through your chosen answer. Finding the way matters as much as knowing the answer.

CHOOSE YOUR ADVENTURE
Children can practice math and selected languages while racing Explorer Fox. A wrong answer reveals colorful glue: lose one life and wriggle for 5 seconds while the rival keeps moving. Looking for a navigation adventure? Explorer mode starts with bigger mazes and questions in science, history, astronomy, general knowledge, programming, arithmetic, mathematics, English, artificial intelligence and paradoxes.
The fox can choose a wrong path and turn back, so following it is not a reliable shortcut. Navigation mode has no racing opponent, and wrong paths run deeper before reaching a dead end.

KEEP EXPLORING
Lose all your lives? Retry the same level from the beginning. In a race, you and the fox restart together. Earlier completed levels and purchases stay safe. Your unfinished adventure is saved automatically. The illustrated journey shows completed levels in blue and Minik at your next step. Replay earlier adventures or let a grown-up reset local progress.

A LITTLE MAGIC
Meet Lumi the magical dog, playful visitors and Balloon Minik. Throw water balloons, explore changing color themes and find temporary light for darker paths. Keep a translucent map open while you move. Adjust controls, graphics and camera comfort in the grown-up area.

FIRST 12 LEVELS FREE
Play without ads. One permanent purchase unlocks levels 13–100. No subscription. Free levels remain replayable and progress stays safe if you choose not to buy. Purchases and external links are behind a grown-up gate.

Questions and answers were created with AI assistance and may contain mistakes. No account, no online profile: progress stays on this device.

QUESTION DIFFICULTY
Automatic difficulty adapts each subject separately: two first-try correct answers raise it one step; two first-try mistakes lower it. A grown-up can turn this off and choose a fixed level for each subject, with examples. Questions already seen stay unchanged. The rival gives a short, chirpy laugh when it overtakes you; turn off Sound for quiet play.
