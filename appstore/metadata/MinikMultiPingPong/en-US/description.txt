More players. More angles. A new way to rally.

Multiplayer Ping Pong brings a colorful twist to table tennis. Play a classic two-player match, try a three-player table or take on the four-sided cross table. Watch the ball, choose your target and turn a simple return into a clever shot.

BUILD YOUR SKILLS, DAY BY DAY
Explore a 30-stage daily skill journey: serve to a called court, aim at golden islands, paint shapes, protect your side and practice team passes and smashes. Earn a bonus for your first completed stage of the day, then keep playing at your own pace. Missing a day never erases your progress. Later cycles develop the same skills with more demanding challenges.

PICK A PARTNER. BUILD THE PLAY.
Team up with a chosen house player for two against one or two against two. Choose your teammate and opponents, pass to your partner and set up a smash. Double-tap to set up your teammate; a well-timed swipe turns a received set into a faster attacking shot. The learning guide and daily challenges help you practice the teamwork.

Choose Team play in a three- or four-player friendly game. Teammates share one score. Friends keep their own nicknames and avatars. The room creator taps players to arrange the teams or add house players; start when all places are filled and selected friends are connected. Team play is available for friendly games; individual tournaments keep Winner takes all and Elimination.

ONE TABLE, MORE POSSIBILITIES
In three- and four-player games, each player has their own arm of the table. Send the ball across the net to another player, switch your target and stay ready for the next return. A clear target indicator helps you follow who is receiving the shot.

CONTROLS THAT GROW WITH YOU
Beginner controls help with the timing: move your paddle into position and it returns the ball automatically. A small sideways swipe chooses who receives it. Standard puts return timing in your hands. Pro adds the challenge of a precise swipe. Choose the control level that feels right and practice at your own pace.

LEARN WITH MINIK
The interactive guide introduces the table, serving, returning and choosing a target. Watch the demonstrations, try the exercises and carry what you learn into a match.

MEET THE HOUSE PLAYERS
Choose your avatar and face a colorful cast including Minik, Miniko, Kyra, Flare, Gaya, Mia and June. Different opponents bring their own playing styles to the table. Start a friendly game with house players for a quick table tennis challenge.

INDIVIDUAL GAME TYPES
Winner takes all: race to the target score. In a three- or four-player rally, a successful shot can add a point to the hitter and cost the receiver a point; ordinary scores stop at zero.
Elimination: stay in the game as players drop out. The table changes with the remaining players, and a classic two-player duel can decide the final winner.

BUILD YOUR OWN TOURNAMENT
Choose a round-robin competition or a knockout tournament. Follow the table assignments, results and progress between rounds. House players can fill the empty places, so you can start a tournament without waiting for a full group.

BRIGHT, PLAYFUL TABLE TENNIS
Expressive characters, colorful tables and clear scores make each match easy to follow. Play a short friendly match or come back to an unfinished competition.

Choose your paddle position. Pick your target. Keep the rally going.

SUNSET RALLIES
Play three- and four-player matches in portrait or landscape on a sunset terrace. Your chosen character joins you at the paddle, with a clear view of the table and scores.
