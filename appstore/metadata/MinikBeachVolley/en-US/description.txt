Minik Beach Volleyball brings the Minik house players to bright beach and park courts. Move into position, receive the ball, set up your teammate and attack an open space. Play 2-on-2 or bring six characters together for a lively 3-on-3 match.

PLAY AS A TEAM
Your house-player teammates choose receiving positions, prepare sets and look for space to attack. Each team has up to three touches. Keep the ball over the net and inside the lines; a ball on the ground gives the other side a point. Different characters bring their own movement, precision and contact qualities.

A FRIENDLY FIRST RALLY
Beginner controls time ball contact when you move within reach. Standard uses a timed tap, while Pro adds swipe-controlled power. Learn to play with Minik in an interactive practice session with no match deadline and no lesson ads.

YOUR CHARACTER, YOUR PROGRESS
Choose a Minik avatar, earn points through good contacts and match wins, and watch your name color advance toward silver and gold. Take on a saved three-match local cup against different house players, or enjoy a single match.

BEACH DAYS AND PARK GAMES
Switch between two colorful settings. Play in portrait or landscape, on phones or tablets. Local games work offline, and unfinished matches can be saved for later.

BUILD YOUR TEAM
Choose every teammate and opponent from 17 house players, including Flash, Finn, Rowan and Diego. Six volleyball skills shape their speed, precision, power, hitting, blocking and serves. Choose Aqua Waves, Golden Suns or the berry-colored team and customize your ball.

SERVE, BLOCK AND SPIKE
Choose Beginner, Standard or Pro serving independently of your rally controls. Practice a timed toss, power a swipe serve, leap at the net to block or dive for a difficult low ball. A double tap can turn a high ball into a spike or lift a low ball for a teammate. Pro shots follow your swipe direction and power, with real chances of net faults and out balls.

A WIDER VIEW
Turn your phone sideways for a broad court with compact coaching beside the action. A refreshed beach, distinct team colors and situation-aware tips help you follow the rally. Meet Kai and Skye, two relaxed surfers with their own playing strengths.

DAILY SKILLS, REAL RALLIES

Practice receiving, moving, blocking and team play in short daily challenges. Build confidence with one skill before combining it with the next.
