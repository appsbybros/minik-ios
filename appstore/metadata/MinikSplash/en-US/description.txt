Find your answer. Pick up a balloon. Make a splash.

Minik Splash combines personal learning questions with a lively balloon battle. Everyone moves at their own pace: solve, collect and throw while the other players keep playing.

ANSWERS LIVE IN THE WORLD
Your question has four answer balloons placed around the arena. Walk to the one you choose and collect it. A correct answer becomes a throw, and your next question starts immediately. In regular battles, a wrong answer bursts in your hand and shows a short correction. In Paint walls, the balloon keeps its color but splashes white paint to erase the other team's paint.

LEARN WHILE YOU MOVE
Start with math, then choose English, world knowledge or a mix of subjects. Practice adapts by skill, and earlier topics return for review. A correct answer still counts when your balloon misses the target.

FIND YOUR CONTROL STYLE
Easy walking takes you to a balloon when you tap it. Standard throwing sends the balloon toward the ground point or player you tap. Prefer a challenge? Hard throwing adds an aiming arc and swipe power. Jump, duck and dodge as the colors fly.

LEARN TO PLAY WITH MINIK
The interactive introduction lets you practice movement, collecting, aiming, throwing, jumping and ducking without a timer. Try the control options and keep the ones you enjoy.

YOUR BATTLE, YOUR TEAM
Choose an individual battle, 2-versus-2 or 3-versus-3, with up to six participants. Team colors, shared scores and individual results make the action easy to follow.

COLORFUL PLACES TO PLAY
Visit the beach, the park and low-gravity Andromeda. Keep answers nearby or scatter them across the arena. Play a single battle or a short cup, and let your growing total points show through your color rank.

House-player play works offline. Questions are practice material, not an educational assessment; AI-assisted content can contain mistakes.

PRACTICE ONE SKILL AT A TIME
The daily paint journey turns movement, aiming, dodging, jumping, ducking and balloon passing into short playable goals. Learn with cooperative partners as well as opponents, then revisit familiar skills with a new challenge. A daily bonus rewards returning; you can keep playing without waiting for tomorrow.

PAINT THE OPPOSING WALL
In Paint walls, your team colors the other team's wall. Correct answer balloons paint in your team color, whatever color the balloon shows. Wrong-answer balloons keep their color and splash white paint to clear the other team's paint. Team scores show wall coverage; in regular team battles, wrong-answer penalties affect the shared score.

CAMERA THAT FOLLOWS THE FUN
Use the camera button to follow your player or see the whole court. Rotate the view and adjust zoom during play. Landscape opens on the wide court.
