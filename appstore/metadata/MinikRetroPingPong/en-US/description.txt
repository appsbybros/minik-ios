Keep the ball bouncing. Catch the next question. Play another round with Minik.

Minik Bounce & Learn combines retro Pong action with playful arithmetic challenges. Move your paddle, face Minik across the court and watch for the question train. Want a livelier game? Switch on Balloon Madness.

SIMPLE CONTROLS, LIVELY RALLIES
Use the on-screen arrows or swipe your paddle to keep the ball in play. Choose Minik's level: Beginner, Medium or Hard. Start gently, then turn up the challenge when you feel ready.

ALL ABOARD THE QUESTION TRAIN
When the train arrives, the action slows down to give you a moment with the question. Tap the wagon showing your answer, then return to the rally. The arithmetic challenge adapts as you answer, bringing addition, subtraction, multiplication and division into the game.

TURN ON BALLOON MADNESS
Balloons, bouncing balls, a little car and other playful objects bring extra surprises to the court. Enjoy the collisions and collect rewards as you play. Prefer a calmer round? Leave Balloon Madness off.

TWO SCORES, TWO REASONS TO PLAY
Win Pong points to decide the match. Rewards from the train and Balloon Madness add only to your total points, so answering a question does not change the match result.

LET YOUR COLORS SHOW YOUR PROGRESS
Your paddle and arrows display your highest unlocked color rank. Start with the wide yellow paddle, then work toward higher ranks with narrower paddles and a little more responsiveness. Minik's paddle always matches yours in length.

PICK UP WHERE YOUR SETTINGS LEFT OFF
Your total points and selected Minik level are saved on your device. Open Help & Settings when you need a reminder and enjoy a layout designed for phone play, including landscape.

A rally, a question and a burst of color. That is Minik Bounce & Learn.
