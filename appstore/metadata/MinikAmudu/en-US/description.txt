A name is called. The ball goes up. Everyone is on the move.

Minik Spud brings a playground ball game to life for the whole family, with colorful characters, throws you can feel and house players with different strengths. Choose your arena, pick a ball and build a game for up to ten players.

CALL, TOSS, CATCH
Choose a player, then swipe upward from the ball in your character's hand. Swipe faster for height, or diagonally to send the ball left or right; wind still affects its path. Run into position and time a catch. A successful catcher takes the next turn while the others can run back toward the circle.

MISS THE CATCH? THE GAME IS STILL ON
Collect the ball, call for everyone to stop when your rules require it, and aim at a player. Runners can try a catch, jump or timed duck. A hit gives the target a penalty; a clean catch or a missed throw gives the thrower a penalty.

CHOOSE YOUR RULES
Play the four-letter SPUD challenge, where penalties build toward sitting out until the next game, or the funny-nickname Stop game. In the nickname game, every three penalties, players vote on a new word: the first word replaces the player's name, and later words make it longer.

A PLAYGROUND OF YOUR OWN
Visit Sunshine Park, the Tropical Beach or low-gravity Andromeda. Try soft foam, beach, neon and tennis balls, or a paint balloon that splashes on uncaught hits or ground misses, and sometimes bursts during a strong catch. Add wind, choose morning, noon, evening or night, and choose your throw controls: Easy tap aiming, Standard moving-target timing, or Pro swipe direction and power. Set the stop-call rule, steps, action time and turn limit to suit the game.

MEET THE HOUSE PLAYERS
Minik, Miniko, Mia, Gaya, Kyra, Flare and their friends each have their own Accuracy, Power, Speed, Catch and Jump ratings. Learn who throws hard, who runs quickly and who is waiting for the right catch.

LEARN, CHOOSE AND PRACTICE
Learn to throw, catch, duck, jump and pass through 40 challenges. Choose Easy, Standard or Pro and a suitable ball, including paint balloons where the task allows them. Complete a challenge once to move on; replay completed stages with different choices without losing progress. Your first newly completed challenge each day earns a bonus. Short lessons explain the controls, the balls, light, wind, gravity, the ground rule, name calls and advanced match options, and let you practice without changing your progress.

INVITE YOUR FRIENDS
Create a private online game and share its six-character room code with the people you want to play with. There is no public matchmaking and no open chat. Mix friends and house players, with each friend using their own nickname and avatar. Online games need internet access; games with house players work offline, and you can save an unfinished local game and return later.

YOUR PRIVATE FAMILY LEAGUE
Keep one family league with the same invitation code and house-player roster. Start a new round when everyone is ready; absent friends sit it out without changing their totals. A win earns three points, with fewer penalty points breaking ties. Follow wins, penalties, games played and nickname history.

FREE TO START, NO ADS
Minik Spud has no ads. Play free with up to 3 players in Sunshine Park, with the soft foam and beach balls and the first 7 challenges; the paint balloon is free for 3 welcome games, then for one game a day. One purchase unlocks the full game for good: up to 10 players, every arena and ball, paint balloons any time and every challenge. Family Sharing is included. In an online room only the host needs the full game, so friends who join never pay. Purchases are protected by a grown-up check.

Play in English, Hebrew, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi or Dutch.
