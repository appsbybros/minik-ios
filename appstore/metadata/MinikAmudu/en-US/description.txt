A name is called. The ball goes up. Everyone is on the move.

Minik Spud brings a playground ball game to life with colorful characters, swipes you can feel and house players with different strengths. Choose your arena, pick a ball and build a game for up to ten participants.

CALL, TOSS, CATCH
Choose a player, then swipe upward from the ball in your character's hand. Your swipe changes the height and speed of the toss. Run into position and time a catch. A successful catcher takes the next turn, with players staying where they reached.

MISS THE CATCH? THE GAME IS STILL ON
Collect the ball, call for everyone to stop when your rules require it, and aim at a player. Runners can try a catch or a timed duck. A hit gives the target a penalty; a clean catch or a missed throw gives the thrower a penalty.

CHOOSE YOUR RULES
Play the four-letter SPUD challenge, where penalties build toward elimination, or the funny-name Stop game. In the nickname game, every three penalties brings a secret word vote: the first word replaces the player's name, and later words make it longer.

A PLAYGROUND OF YOUR OWN
Visit the park, the beach or low-gravity Andromeda. Try the soft foam, beach, neon party and tennis balls. Add wind, choose morning, noon, evening or night, and experiment with easy or standard throwing. Set the stop-call rule, steps, action time and turn limit to suit the game.

MEET THE HOUSE PLAYERS
Minik, Miniko, Mia, Gaya, Kyra, Flare and their friends each have their own Accuracy, Power, Speed and Catch ratings. Learn who throws hard, who runs quickly and who is waiting for the right catch.

Play in portrait or landscape, save an unfinished local game and return later. House-player games work offline.

A LITTLE SKILL, EVERY DAY
Practice throwing, catching, ducking, jumping and accurate passes in a daily skill journey. Short goals introduce the controls before combining them into more demanding challenges. Return for a daily bonus or keep practicing at your own pace.

INVITE YOUR FRIENDS
Mix friends and house players, with each friend using their own nickname and avatar.
