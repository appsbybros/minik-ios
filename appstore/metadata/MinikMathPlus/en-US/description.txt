Make room for a little math adventure!

Minik Math Plus brings numbers to life with colorful pictures, hands-on activities and playful challenges. Count objects, build a number, match an exercise to its answer, or kick the correct answer into the goal.

LEARN, PRACTICE AND PLAY
• Learn Math: meet each fact with pictures, then review the whole table.
• Build Number and Build Quantity: put numbers together, one piece at a time.
• Picture to answer and answer to picture: connect quantities and numbers both ways.
• Math Pairs and Math Memory: find the matching values.
• Build Math and Mixed practice: put answers together and try a bit of everything.
• Math Soccer: kick the right answer into the goal.
• Math Tower: build the target value block by block.
• A table tennis break against Minik.

TEN LEVELS TO EXPLORE
Start with counting and quantities, then move on to addition, subtraction, place value, multiplication and division. Later levels bring fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, missing numbers and simple equations.

Choose a level yourself or let automatic progression follow recent answers. Points and best streaks celebrate the practice along the way.

A DAILY MATH ADVENTURE
Follow a daily route through different kinds of practice, from matching pictures and building numbers to soccer and towers. Reach the answer goal, collect stars and continue the journey.

Optional spoken prompts read the questions aloud next to the pictures and instructions. English and Hebrew, with right-to-left layouts for Hebrew.

Pick an activity, try an answer and keep exploring with Minik!
