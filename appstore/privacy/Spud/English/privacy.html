Privacy policy

Spud is published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling. Optional services operate only when available and enabled in your installed version.

On your device

Your game name, avatar, rules, settings, saved local match, challenge progress and color points stay on your device. The app also remembers on the device whether the full game is unlocked, how many of the free paint-balloon games have been used and the day of the last free one. A real name, email or birth date is not required. Local games against house players do not upload match state to our game database.

Online play and sharing

Online play happens only in private rooms and family leagues. There is no public matchmaking: a room is joined only with its six-character code, which players share outside the app. When you open or join a room, Firebase anonymous authentication assigns a random user ID without email or password registration. Private-room data includes the room code, names, avatars, language setting, readiness/presence, rules and settings, movements, ball actions, penalties, nicknames and results. Other participants see the information needed to play; one player’s device, normally that of the player who opened the room, processes the game state. Typed one-word nickname suggestions and votes go to the room; eligible participants see them during the huddle, excluding the target until the chosen name is revealed. This game rule cannot stop other people repeating or capturing a suggestion. Players can send a few preset messages, which may be shown or spoken to the group. There is no open chat. Anyone with a code may join a room still accepting players, so share codes only with people you know.

A family league additionally keeps its invitation code, the anonymous ID of the player who created it, the member roster (names, avatars and language setting), the league’s game settings, the code of a round in progress and, for each finished round, its participants, winners, penalty points and the nicknames they earned. From these the app shows wins, points, penalty points, games played and nickname history. Only joined participants receive a round’s result; absent members stay in the roster.

No ads

Spud shows no ads and contains no advertising code. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, has no access to your device’s advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Full game and purchase

Spud is free to start. Without the full game, a game has up to 3 players, the Sunshine park arena, the Soft foam and Beach ball balls, the paint balloon for 3 welcome games and then for one game a day, and the first 7 challenges. One purchase through the App Store, with no subscription, unlocks the full game; Family Sharing is included. Buying requires a grown-up check; Restore purchases does not. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card or Apple Account details. The app checks with Apple on the device whether the full game is owned (including through Family Sharing, refunds and purchases waiting for a parent’s approval) and remembers the result on the device. The paint-balloon counter exists only on the device. In an online room only the player who opens it needs the full game; the room receives only the settings that player chose, not purchase information. There are no access codes, and we have no purchase server of our own.

Children and parents

Spud is made for the whole family; younger children should play with an adult nearby. A grown-up check (a multiplication question) protects the purchase button, Settings & feedback (rating, sharing, feedback and the lag diagnosis) and links that leave the app, such as the More Minik apps & games card and the links in this policy. Adults should supervise purchases and private-group play. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data at any time.

Permissions and speech

No camera, microphone, contacts or location permission is requested. Avatars are supplied artwork, not uploaded photos. During a game, the app reads called names, the stop call, new nicknames and preset messages aloud using Apple’s built-in speech voices on your device. We do not record your voice. The app uses the internet for online play, App Store purchases, the More Minik apps & games list and, only if you start it, the lag diagnosis. Share the game opens the system share sheet and Send feedback opens your own email app; nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Optional online-lag diagnosis

An adult can explicitly start a ten-minute support session in Settings & feedback and stop it at any time. It sends aggregate frame and connection timings, the app version and build, the device model, the interface language, the role in the room (host or guest), data-size totals and error categories to Firebase under the app’s anonymous user ID and a random report ID. It does not record names, room codes, camera images, audio, individual gestures or gameplay video. Other players cannot read the report. Collection stops after ten minutes or when switched off. Reports are marked for support cleanup after one day; deletion currently happens during manual support cleanup, not automatically at that time.

Providers and security

Google provides Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) for online rooms, family leagues, the lag diagnosis and the More Minik apps & games list; Apple provides the App Store, in-app purchases and the speech voices. The apps list is downloaded at most once a day, without any player ID or game data. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Firebase connections use encrypted transport; this is not end-to-end encryption against the game’s authority device. Providers may process data outside your country. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or any advertising or tracking component. We do not sell player profiles or learning records, and we do not share data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local settings, progress and saves, the full-game flag and the paint-balloon counter stay on your device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. The purchase itself stays with your Apple Account and can be restored after reinstalling. The app removes unused lobbies and completed rooms when the cleanup conditions are met, and registers conditional disconnect cleanup. This is not a universal scheduled expiry: interrupted connections or older versions can leave records requiring support cleanup. A family league’s roster and results stay between games until the player who created it deletes the league in the app; an active round must finish first. Deleting the app does not erase cloud data and can lose the anonymous identity used to reach it; contact us before deleting the app if you want cloud deletion. There is no account-deletion button, because there is no account beyond the anonymous ID. Ask support to delete relevant records. Security and support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational/legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

Use a nickname instead of a real name; share room codes only with trusted people. Do not enter contact details or sensitive information into game text. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using available privacy options or by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
