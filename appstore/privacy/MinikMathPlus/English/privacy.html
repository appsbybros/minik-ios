Minik Math Plus is a math-learning app for children, published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling.

On your device

Minik Math Plus keeps your child’s learning data on the device: the math level and whether it is chosen automatically or manually, practice statistics (such as sessions, correct and incorrect answers, skips and practice time), ping pong points, progress and settings, the app language and the reminder setting. It also stores ad-frequency counters and whether Remove Ads is active. The app does not ask for your child’s name, email address, phone number, birth date, photo or location, and has no free-text profile.

Online services

Minik Math Plus has no account, online leaderboard or online play, and it does not use Firebase. Its ping pong games run on the device; the classic ping pong game runs from files inside the app in a built-in web view and loads no web pages. The app connects to the internet only for ads and App Store purchases; the privacy, terms and support links that a parent opens after a grown-up check open outside the app.

Learning reminders

Learning reminders are off by default. If a parent turns them on in Parent Area, the app asks for notification permission at that moment and schedules one local reminder a week on the device (for example, “Time for Minik”). Reminders are not sent from a server, and no push-notification service is used. They can be turned off in Parent Area or in the device’s Settings app.

Speech and permissions

Math questions and instructions are read aloud using Apple’s built-in speech voices on the device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record your child’s voice. No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission is requested; the only permission the app may ask for is notifications, and only when a parent turns reminders on.

Ads and children

Minik Math Plus shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob at breaks after a finished math round or ping pong match, never in the middle of one, and no more than once every few minutes. Every ad request is marked child-directed and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalized ads and limits ads to a G rating. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification.

The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the app, including Google’s ad code in it, has no access to the device’s advertising identifier. This does not make advertising data-free: Google may still process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app stores ad-frequency counters on the device. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Buying or restoring it requires a grown-up check in Parent Area. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card details. The app checks the purchase with Apple on the device and remembers on the device that ads are removed; Restore Purchases recovers it on the same Apple Account. We have no purchase server of our own. While ads are shown, a Remove Ads reminder for parents may appear, at most once every 14 days; buying from it also requires the grown-up check.

Children and parents

Minik Math Plus is made for children. A grown-up check (solving an addition problem) protects purchases, restoring purchases and links that leave the app (privacy policy, terms of use and support). A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data at any time.

Providers and security

Google provides Google AdMob for ads; Apple provides the App Store and purchases. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Connections use encrypted transport. Providers may process data outside your country. The app does not include Firebase, analytics, crash reporting or remote push notifications. We do not sell your child’s data or learning records.

Retention and deletion

Learning progress, settings and other local data stay on the device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. Minik Math Plus sends no learning or game data to us. Support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational or legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

You can leave reminders off and buy Remove Ads to stop ads. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using the settings in Parent Area or by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

Contact and policy changes

Apps By Bros — appsbybros.dev@gmail.com. To request help, identify the app; never send passwords or full payment-card details. We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
