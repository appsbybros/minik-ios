Privacy policy

Minik Math is a math practice game for children, with a ping pong game, published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling.

On your device

Your practice level, the result of the last finished round, your ping pong points, levels and settings, and the score of the last finished ping pong game are stored on the device. The app also stores ad-frequency counters and whether Remove Ads is active. No name, email address or birth date is asked for, and there is no free-text profile.

The game and the internet

The game runs from files inside the app in a built-in web view and loads no web pages or online content. The link to our website at the bottom of the home screen opens in Safari only after a grown-up check. Minik Math has no account, online play or leaderboard, and it uses the internet only for ads and App Store purchases.

Ads and children

Minik Math shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob only after a finished round of questions or a finished ping pong game, never during play. The first two finished rounds never show an ad, and ads are at least two minutes apart. Every ad request is marked child-directed and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalized ads and limits ads to a G rating. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification.

The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the app, including Google’s ad code in it, has no access to the device’s advertising identifier. This does not make advertising data-free: Google may still process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app stores ad-frequency counters on the device. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The Parents · Remove ads link on the home screen opens a grown-up check before Remove Ads, Restore purchase and the privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card details. The app checks the purchase with Apple on the device and remembers on the device that ads are removed; Restore purchases recovers it on the same Apple Account. We have no purchase server of our own.

Permissions and sound

No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission is requested. The app plays game sounds and music but does not read text aloud and does not record your voice. It sends no notifications.

Children and parents

Minik Math is designed with children in mind. A grown-up check (solving an addition problem) protects purchases, restoring purchases and the link to our website. The app has no online play, chat or account. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data at any time.

Providers and security

Google provides Google AdMob for ads; Apple provides the App Store and purchases. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Connections use encrypted transport. Providers may process data outside your country. The app does not include Firebase, analytics, crash reporting or remote push notifications. We do not sell your child’s data or game records.

Retention and deletion

Practice levels, points, settings and other local data stay on the device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. Minik Math sends no game data to us. Support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational or legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

You can buy Remove Ads to stop ads. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

Contact and policy changes

Apps By Bros — appsbybros.dev@gmail.com. To request help, identify the app; never send passwords or full payment-card details. We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
