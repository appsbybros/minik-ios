Privacy policy

Minik Maze

Apps By Bros publishes Minik Maze for Android (com.appsbybros.minik.maze). Updated 13 October 2026. Questions or deletion requests: appsbybros.dev@gmail.com.

Progress, settings, unfinished adventures and difficulty adjustments are stored on your device. No real name, email address, contacts, microphone, camera or precise location is needed to play. Uninstalling clears local game data; the app does not enable Android cloud backup.

Online records are optional. Opening them uses Firebase anonymous authentication, which creates a random account identifier. Sharing requires the grown-up gate and a 1–3 character alias. Only that alias, per-level best scores and their total are submitted to Firebase Realtime Database. Other players can view the limited ranking. The Remove my online record action deletes that record; local progress remains. If the app was uninstalled first, contact us with the alias and available account identifier. No analytics SDK is included.

The shared Minik catalogue downloads editorial app descriptions and artwork and caches them locally. Its requests contain no player account or game progress, but the service receives normal connection information such as IP address. Outbound links require the grown-up gate. This website has no tracking scripts, analytics or advertising cookies; the hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs.

This version contains no advertising SDK and displays no ads or automatic promotions. The first 12 levels are free. One permanent Google Play purchase unlocks the remaining levels; there is no subscription. Resetting local progress does not remove ownership. Optional More Minik apps links remain in the grown-up area.

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The app receives product, purchase-token and ownership information to restore and acknowledge purchases; it does not receive payment-card details. We do not sell player records or ask children for real names. Contact us to ask about access or deletion. Google’s handling is described in its privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.
