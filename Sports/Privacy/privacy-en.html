Privacy policy

Minik Squash and Minik Beach Volley are published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes these Android apps. Updated 6 October 2026.

Settings, selected avatar, saved local matches, color-rank points, cup results and Squash learning progress are stored on your device. We do not request a real name, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. There is no open chat.

When online play is configured and you enter a private room, Firebase Anonymous Authentication assigns an account identifier. Firebase Realtime Database stores the room code, selected avatar/name, ready/connection state, match inputs, positions, scores, learning state and results so participants can play and reconnect. Participants in that room can see the shared game data. Do not share a room code publicly. Production online play is not activated in the current development build.

Public tournaments can be discovered and joined by anyone signed in anonymously. Their public listing contains the room code, match count, capacity and occupied places, not personal learning records. Once someone joins, they can access the same shared match state as other room participants, including learning state. Private-room codes should still be shared only with intended players.

Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools may process IP address, device/app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics when advertising is enabled. Ads are requested with child-directed, under-age and non-personalized settings and a G content rating. Advertising-ID permissions are removed. These settings do not mean the ad service processes no data. Debug versions use Google test ads; production ad IDs are not configured. Lessons and active matches do not display interstitial ads.

Purchases, when configured, are processed by Google Play. The app and its verification service use purchase tokens and entitlement receipts to validate ad removal; we do not receive card details. Code redemption sends the entered app-specific code for verification. Purchase/code services are not activated in this development version.

Local data remains until removed from the device. Online game data, when enabled, remains until deleted by the operator; automatic retention expiry is not implemented. To request access or deletion, contact appsbybros.dev@gmail.com with the app name and room code if available. Do not send a child’s real name or sensitive information. Removing the app does not automatically delete cloud records. Parents may contact us about a child’s data.

We use service providers for game synchronization, advertising and purchases. Production service connections use encrypted transport; local development emulators are only used for testing. We do not operate a sale-of-personal-data service. No system is perfectly secure. Changes to this policy will be posted with an updated date.
